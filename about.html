When I was in P5 at my primary school, I went to some coding club at the town's arts & cultural centre. There was the place I learned about Makecode Arcade. After that, I got an idea... that I was going to make video games for other people's entertainment. That was when I started making video games at home. I never knew I would be here by now, because i'm still only in primary school. But, now I have a new video game every 2 weeks to let people play in our school playground. I still only have me, my knowledge and my imagination, but only knowledge and imagination is key to success.
